## 🌍 About the Project

TourEase is a smart tourism platform designed to transform how people explore local destinations, especially the hidden gems of the Garhwal and Kumaon regions of Uttarakhand.

Traditional travel planning often requires switching between multiple platforms to find accommodation, local guides, transportation and authentic experiences. Meanwhile, many small tourism businesses and lesser-known destinations remain digitally invisible.

TourEase aims to solve this problem by bringing the entire local tourism ecosystem onto one platform.

Using AI-powered recommendation concepts and personalized trip planning, TourEase aims to help travellers discover destinations and experiences based on their budget, available days, interests and location while providing digital visibility to local tourism providers.

**Our vision: Make local tourism more accessible, discoverable and sustainable.**

## 💡 Our Proposed Solution

TourEase proposes a unified tourism ecosystem that connects travellers directly with local tourism services.

### Key Features

- **AI-Based Trip Planner:** Personalized itinerary generation based on budget, days, interests and location.
- **Destination Discovery:** Explore popular as well as hidden and lesser-known destinations.
- **Local Stay Discovery:** Find homestays, hotels and guesthouses.
- **Local Guide Network:** Connect travellers with local guides and authentic experiences.
- **Transport Discovery:** Find local transportation options.
- **Budget-Friendly Planning:** Recommendations based on the traveller's spending preferences.
- **Local Business Visibility:** Help small tourism providers reach more travellers.
- **Low-Data Friendly Experience:** Lightweight design for areas with limited network connectivity.
- **Feedback-Based Recommendations:** Use traveller reviews and feedback to improve future recommendations.

## ⚙️ How TourEase Works

1. **User Input:** Traveller enters budget, number of days, interests and preferred region.
2. **AI Matching:** The proposed recommendation engine filters destinations and local services according to user preferences.
3. **Itinerary Generation:** A personalized travel route with suggested activities and stays is generated.
4. **Choose & Connect:** Travellers compare available options and contact or book local providers.
5. **Feedback & Improvement:** Reviews and travel feedback help improve future recommendations.

  - Garhwal High Hills

  - Offbeat Village Circuits

- Adjustable daily budget.
- Multiple travel interests.
- Dynamic itinerary preview based on selected preferences.
- Local tourism ecosystem overview.
- Implementation roadmap and impact sections.

**Note:** The current itinerary generator uses predefined JavaScript activity data to demonstrate the planning experience. Live AI integration, real-time provider availability, database connectivity and booking functionality are part of the proposed development roadmap, not currently implemented features.
